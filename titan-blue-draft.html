The Titan Blue

Not too sweet. Not too sour. All Titans.

Kyle's Signature Drink

This is our drink. Crisp, cold, and Titans blue — built for tailgates, watch parties, and fourth quarter comebacks. Not some sugary Kool-Aid blue. Not a sour punch in the face. This one hits right. Clean vodka, real citrus, just enough blue curaçao to get the color without turning it into candy. Club soda keeps it light and drinkable.

The Ingredients

  • Vodka — 1.5–2 oz (Tito's, Deep Eddy, or any clean vodka)
  • Blue Curaçao — 1 oz (this is the Titans blue)
  • Fresh lemon juice — 3/4 oz (real lemons, no bottled stuff)
  • Fresh lime juice — 1/2 oz (balances the sweet)
  • Simple syrup — 1/4 oz (light hand — curaçao already has sweetness)
  • Club soda — 2–3 oz splash (the backbone)
  • Ice — crushed for frozen, cubed for shaken
  • Garnish — lemon wheel + fresh mint sprig

Use fresh citrus. This drink lives or dies on real lemon and lime.

Four Ways to Mix It

01

The Classic Titan

Clean & Balanced
2 oz vodka, 1 oz blue curaçao, 3/4 oz fresh lemon, 1/2 oz fresh lime, 1/4 oz simple syrup. Shake hard with ice for 15 seconds. Strain into a rocks glass over fresh ice. Top with a splash of club soda. Garnish with a lemon wheel.
The one that started it all. Crisp, balanced, perfectly blue. This is the standard.
Medium — Smooth Sipper
02

Titan Cooler

Tall & Refreshing
1.5 oz vodka, 1 oz blue curaçao, 1/2 oz fresh lemon, 1/4 oz simple syrup. Build in a tall glass over ice. Top with 4–5 oz club soda. Stir gently twice. Add a mint sprig and lemon wedge.
All-day sessionable. Light enough to drink through four quarters without going down.
Light — All-Day Drinker
03

Frozen Titan

Blended & Bold
2 oz vodka, 1.5 oz blue curaçao, 1 oz fresh lemon, 1/2 oz fresh lime, 1/4 oz simple syrup, 2 cups ice. Blend until smooth and slushy. Pour into a chilled pint glass. Lemon wheel on the rim.
September heat at Nissan Stadium? This is the move. Cold, blue, dangerous.
Strong — Sneaky Smooth
04

Titan Blue Batch

Party Pitcher — Serves 8
12 oz vodka (1.5 cups), 8 oz blue curaçao (1 cup), 6 oz fresh lemon juice, 4 oz fresh lime juice, 2 oz simple syrup. Stir together in a pitcher with ice. Add 16 oz club soda right before serving. Pour over ice in each glass, garnish each one.
The whole tailgate is drinking blue. Mix it up, set it out, Titan Up.
Crowd Pleaser — Makes a Full Pitcher

The Rules

  • Fresh citrus only. Bottled lemon juice will make this taste like a cleaning product. Squeeze your own.
  • Go easy on the syrup. Blue curaçao already has sweetness built in. The 1/4 oz is there to round it out, not make it candy.
  • Don't skip the soda. The club soda opens it up and keeps it from being heavy. That's what makes it drinkable.
  • Cold glass matters. Throw your glasses in the freezer 20 minutes before. The drink hits different when the glass is frosted.
  • Shake it like you mean it. For the Classic and Frozen mixes — a lazy shake gives you a lazy drink. 15 seconds minimum.
