Blue Crew Drumline

The Official Titans Drumline

The Blue Crew is the official drumline of the Tennessee Titans, bringing high-energy percussion performances to every home game at Nissan Stadium. They set the tempo for gameday with thundering beats that echo through the stands.

The Blue Crew performs during pregame tailgates, timeouts, and halftime, collaborating with the cheerleaders and T-Rac to keep the crowd engaged. Their signature drum cadences have become a beloved part of the Titans gameday experience.

Featuring Chloe Holladay — the first Titans majorette and first NFL baton twirler since 2002. Former Alabama Crimsonette.
