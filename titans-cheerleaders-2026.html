Blue Crew

The Official Titans Drumline

The Blue Crew is the official drumline of the Tennessee Titans, bringing high-energy percussion performances to every home game at Nissan Stadium. Founded alongside the cheerleading program, they set the tempo for gameday with thundering beats that echo through the stands.

The Blue Crew performs during pregame, timeouts, and halftime, collaborating with the cheerleaders and T-Rac to keep the crowd engaged throughout the game. Their signature drum cadences have become a beloved part of the Titans gameday experience.
