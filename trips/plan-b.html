Real Talk: It's Hot

Nashville in August averages 87F daytime, 68F overnight, with 73% humidity. That's a real-feel of about 100F at midday. The good news: preseason kickoffs are evening (7pm CT), when temps drop to mid-70s, and late August is the driest 10-day window of the year. Pack lightweight clothes, sunscreen, and a portable fan. Plan indoor activities for midday.

Hot Weather Trip Tips

>
Pack lightweight, breathable fabrics - cotton, linen. Skip jeans for Broadway.
>
Hydrate. Then hydrate again. Heat index hits 100F midday.
>
Plan indoor activities (museums, Hall of Fame) for noon-4pm. Outdoor stuff before 11am or after 6pm.
>
Wear navy/light blue or red. Skip visiting team gear in Nissan Stadium.
>
"Titan Up!" is the call. "Ooh! Ooh! Ooh!" three times after every Titans first down.
>
Pedestrian bridge is the gameday transport hack. Skip $40+ parking.
>
Final season at original Nissan Stadium. Grab a souvenir that marks it.
