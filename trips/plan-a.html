Full-scale replica. This is WHY the Titans are named the Titans - Nashville is the "Athens of the South." Required stop for a super fan.

Live Music Hit List

Robert's Western World - Most authentic honky-tonk on Broadway. Free.
Tootsie's Orchid Lounge - Where Willie got discovered. Three floors, three bands.
Grand Ole Opry - Tues/Fri/Sat. Bucket-list. $50-150. Book 30+ days out.
Bluebird Cafe - Where Taylor Swift was found. Reservations Mondays at 8am, 1 week ahead.
Station Inn - Bluegrass mecca, Gulch. Cash only. Sunday Night Jam free.
The 5 Spot - East Nashville locals' joint. No tourists.

Super Fan Trip Tips

>
Wear navy/light blue or red. Skip visiting team gear in Nissan Stadium.
>
"Titan Up!" is the call. "Ooh! Ooh! Ooh!" three times after every Titans first down.
>
Pedestrian bridge from downtown = the gameday transport hack. Skip $40+ parking entirely.
>
This is the FAREWELL season at original Nissan Stadium (new one opens 2027). Buy a souvenir that marks it.
>
Tip honky-tonk bands $5-10 cash. They play for tips. Nashville code.
>
The Parthenon visit isn't optional - the team is named for Nashville being the "Athens of the South."
